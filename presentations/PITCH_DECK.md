## Slide 2: The Problem
**Headline:** Skilled Artisans Are Invisible & Underserved

*   **Limited Exposure:** talented local artisans (carpenters, welders, plumbers) struggle to find new clients outside their immediate circle.
*   **Operational Inefficiency:** Manual invoicing, lack of inventory tracking, and poor financial management stunt growth.
*   **Skill Gap:** Access to modern techniques, blueprints, and upskilling resources is disconnected and expensive.
*   **Pricing Guesswork:** Without data, artisans underprice their work or lose bids by overpricing.

---

## Slide 3: The Solution
**Headline:** A Digital Ecosystem for the Modern Artisan

**Osun Artisan Hub** is an all-in-one mobile platform designed to professionalize and elevate the informal artisan sector.

*   **Business in a Box:** Complete Business Suite for invoicing, inventory, and expense tracking.
*   **Opportunity Engine:** Direct connection to local leads and job opportunities.
*   **Knowledge Base:** Integrated Learning Management System (LMS) for upskilling.
*   **Intelligent Assistant:** AI-driven insights for pricing and market trends.

---

## Slide 4: Key Features
**Headline:** Built for Growth

1.  **Digital Tools & Blueprints**  
    *   Specific tools for trades (e.g., measurement calculators for carpenters).
    *   Access to a library of professional blueprints.

2.  **Job Connect**  
    *   Real-time job leads from local clients.
    *   Instant quoting and application secure messaging.

3.  **Skills Training**  
    *   Video courses on modern techniques (e.g., "Advanced Joinery").
    *   Certification tracking to boost credibility.

4.  **Portfolio Management**  
    *   Digital showcase of past work to build trust with potential clients.

---

## Slide 5: The "Secret Sauce" (AI & Analytics)
**Headline:** Data-Driven Empowerment

Unlike incredible basic directories, Osun Artisan Hub uses **AI Insights** to guide business decisions:

*   **Dynamic Pricing:** "Carpentry services in your area are charging 15% more—consider updating your rates."
*   **Demand Forecasting:** "High demand for welders in Osogbo Central this week."
*   **Performance Metrics:** Real-time visibility into Revenue, Expenses, and Job Completion rates.

---

## Slide 6: Market Opportunity
**Headline:** Tapping into the Informal Economy

*   **The Market:** Millions of skilled artisans in the informal economy.
*   **The Trend:** Increasing smartphone penetration and internet accessibility among tradespeople.
*   **The Gap:** Existing platforms are either focused on white-collar freelance (Upwork) or are simple classifieds (Jiji/OLX) without business management tools.

---

## Slide 7: Business Model
**Headline:** Sustainable Revenue Streams

1.  **Freemium Model:** Basic profile and job browsing are free to aggregate supply.
2.  **Premium Subscription (Business Suite):** Monthly fee for invoicing, unlimited inventory, and deep analytics.
3.  **Training Marketplace:** Commission on paid courses and certifications.
4.  **Job Success Fee:** Small commission on jobs secured through the platform (Future roadmap).

---

## Slide 8: Technology Stack
**Headline:** Robust, Scalable, & Real-Time

*   **Frontend:** React Native (Expo) & TypeScript – Cross-platform (iOS/Android) with a native feel.
*   **Styling:** NativeWind (Tailwind CSS) – Beautiful, modern, and responsive UI.
*   **Backend:** Node.js & Express – Scalable REST API.
*   **Database:** MongoDB – Flexible schema for diverse artisan profiles and job types.
*   **Real-Time:** Socket.io – Instant messaging between artisans and clients.
